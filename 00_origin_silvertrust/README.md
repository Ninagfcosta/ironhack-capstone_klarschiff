# 00 · Origin: where the KlarSchiff idea was born 🌱

KlarSchiff started in **Project 4 · SilverTrust**, a pair consulting exercise at Ironhack.
My classmate **Asal** played the client for the **logistics** sector and I was her **AI consultant**.
In the client interview she described a real pain point: shipment documents are checked by hand and customs errors are only found at the border.

This folder keeps the original deliverables of that exercise:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `logistics-brief.pdf` | The consulting brief for the logistics client |
| `hoffmann_consultant_solutions.pptx` | My first solution deck: the 5-step pre-shipment workflow |

**From seed to product:** the problem, the first numbers (illustrative) and the 5-step workflow came from here. The capstone then turned them into KlarSchiff: research, charts, an n8n proof of concept and a scored evaluation.

Full module: [ironhack-module-4_evaluation-compliance_silvertrust](https://github.com/ninagfcosta/ironhack-module-4_evaluation-compliance_silvertrust)
