# Roadmap to production (what is NOT done in the capstone, and when)

| Item | Why it matters | When | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blind test on real, anonymised data with a licensed broker | Our scores come from cases we wrote (E6) | Pilot week 2 | broker time |
| 10-digit TARIC (EU) and full US HTS statistical suffixes | Declarations need the full national code | Pilot | free official data |
| Chapter and section notes, explanatory notes in the prompt | Parts and multi-function machines follow legal notes | After pilot | WCO notes are licensed |
| HS 2028 change-over | The WCO HS changes every ~5 years | 2027 | free |
| ISO 27001 / SOC 2 controls, SSO, penetration test | Large customers require them before production | Product phase | paid |
| Integration API with the customer's TMS / ERP | Manual upload does not scale | After a "go" | project |
| Model risk management: re-evaluate on every model change | Model updates can change answers | Continuous | tokens |
