# Timeline Estimate — Pilot Delivery Plan

A 1-to-3-month pilot, run in short sprints, scoped to one route and one product family (per `use_cases.md` pilot scope).

## Roles
- **Product Owner:** logistics team lead (I&E LLC)
- **Scrum Master / delivery lead:** Janaina Hoffmann
- **Delivery team:** AI implementation team

## Sprint plan

| Sprint | Timing | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 0 | Month 1 | Discovery | Map the route, documents, roles, and baseline processing time |
| Sprint 1 | Month 1 | Build | Prepare product data; build the first n8n prototype |
| Sprint 2 | Month 2 | Prototype test | Test against real historical shipments (anonymized) |
| Sprint 3 | Month 2–3 | Live pilot | Controlled test with the team, fully human-reviewed |
| Sprint 4 | Month 3 | Review | Decide on results, gaps, and next steps |

Each sprint ends with a short review and retrospective, so the plan adjusts if something isn't working — this is stated explicitly to the client so the timeline reads as realistic, not a fixed promise.

## Pilot scope boundaries (kept deliberately small)

| Dimension | Scope |
|---|---|
| Route | One route only (e.g., a representative international lane I&E LLC already ships) |
| Product family | One construction-material product family |
| Volume | 50 to 100 historical shipment line items |
| Sources | Official tariff sources, with links kept for every review |
| Filing | Customs filing stays fully manual throughout the pilot |

## Assumptions

- I&E LLC can provide anonymized historical shipment samples (invoices, packing lists, product descriptions) at Sprint 0.
- Approved HS codes and past broker corrections are available, if they exist, to validate suggestions against.
- No ERP/customs-system integration is in scope for this pilot — that is quoted separately, after the pilot, per `cost_analysis.md`.
